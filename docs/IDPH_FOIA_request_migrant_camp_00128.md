# Draft Illinois Department of Public Health FOIA request

Status: Draft only - not submitted

Submission guidance: [IDPH Freedom of Information Act page](https://dph.illinois.gov/about/freedom-of-information-act.html)

## Subject

FOIA request - migrant labor camp 00128, Fiesta Motel, Pontiac

## Draft request

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following noncommercial public records for Illinois Department of Public Health migrant labor camp license/permit 00128, identified in the public state roster as Fiesta Motel, 951 W. Reynolds St., Pontiac, IL 61764, operated by Three G Trucking LLC, for the period January 1, 2018 through the date this request is processed:

1. All inspection reports, inspection checklists, field notes, photographs, and supporting records.
2. All notices of violation or deficiency, corrective-action plans, compliance correspondence, reinspection reports, and records documenting correction or closure of a finding.
3. All permit or license applications, renewals, approvals, denials, suspensions, variances, and related correspondence.
4. All complaints and complaint-investigation records concerning sanitation, laundry, washing, water supply, or worker housing conditions.
5. Records specifically documenting compliance with 77 Ill. Adm. Code 935.65(d), including the presence, number, operability, and hot- and cold-water supply of laundry trays, tubs, or mechanical washing machines; the occupancy used for the required-facility calculation; and any variance or alternative compliance determination.

Please provide the records electronically in their native format or as searchable PDFs. This request does not seek Social Security numbers, medical information, or other personal information exempt from disclosure. If a record contains exempt material, please redact only that material and release the remainder. If any portion is denied, please identify the specific statutory basis and the records withheld. If fees are expected, please provide an estimate before processing charges above $25.

Please also advise if another state or local agency is the custodian of any responsive inspection or licensing records.

Thank you.
